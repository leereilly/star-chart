StarChartSymbols-Regular.ttf

This tiny fallback font supplies only the star glyphs U+2605 (★ BLACK STAR)
and U+2606 (☆ WHITE STAR), which the bundled Roboto font does not include. It
exists so the GIF rasteriser renders the chart's header total and logo instead
of a missing-glyph box (tofu).

The outlines are original, self-authored five-pointed stars generated by
scripts/build-symbol-font.py. This font is dedicated to the public domain under
